# 💭 Thoughts Log

One line per thought: `- YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM · **Project** · the thought`. Time and project are optional. Big thoughts can have their own note: link it with `[[Note name]]`. Homebase's **Thoughts** page shows this log as a timeline, together with `02 Inbox/`, `40 Ideas/` and the rants in your daily notes.

Where did your projects come from? Add their first thoughts here, even old ones, so you can see how it all started.
